%%%%
%%%% sgp_cdf_identity.tex -- PSTricks schematic for the RIDE TAC deck.
%%%%
%%%% Shows that the cross-sectional CDF comparison of Ho (2009) is the
%%%% zero-covariate case of the baseline SGP:
%%%%
%%%%   A. Condition on the prior score -> baseline SGP        F_b(y | x)
%%%%   B. Drop the conditioning        -> marginal percentile F_b(y)
%%%%   C. Average over the cohort      -> AUC = P(Y_b < Y_c), the area
%%%%                                     ABOVE the PP curve
%%%%
%%%% Build notes (see build_pstricks.R in this directory):
%%%%   * `algebraic` plotting lives in pstricks-add, not pst-plot.
%%%%   * pst-algparser maps exp() to PostScript's two-operand power operator;
%%%%     EXP() is the natural exponential. Its trig functions take RADIANS.
%%%%   * The pspicture box reserves its own vertical space -- no \vspace.
%%%%   * `opacity` does not survive the dvips -> gs pdfwrite chain, so
%%%%     translucent fills are expressed as explicit pale colours.
%%%%
